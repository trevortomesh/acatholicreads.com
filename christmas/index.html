How It Works

Advent, kept at the Church's pace

The Sundays are kept on Mondays. The group meets on weeknights, so each Sunday of Advent — its Mass readings and its collect — opens the first session of its week. The season loses nothing to the weekend.

A Catholic thread runs through it. A Father speaks once a week: St. Bernard's three comings open the season, St. Irenaeus joins the Immaculate Conception, and St. Leo the Great has the last word of the year. Mary carries the human thread — December 8, Guadalupe, the Visitation, the Annunciation — and the final week is prayed the Church's way, with the O Antiphons and the Magnificat from the Liturgy of the Hours.

The finale is December 23: "The Night Without Darkness" — the last antiphon, O Emmanuel, then 3 Nephi 1 and Luke 2 side by side: the sign in the Americas and the birth at Bethlehem, in one night.
